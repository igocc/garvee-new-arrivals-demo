Why this structure

One product pool, four useful reasons to browse

01

Category appears once

Top-level tabs replace the repeated category shelf pattern. Subcategories become lightweight filters when needed.

02

“Hot” has guardrails

Velocity alone is not enough. The model includes freshness, review confidence, inventory depth, and delivery readiness.

03

Deals stay credible

Discount sorting is separated from trending. Production should validate price history and minimum margin before merchandising.

04

Mobile starts with products

No email gate and no oversized promotional banner. The first swipe reaches category and product discovery immediately.

The demo score is directional, not a claim about live Garvee performance. In production, each input needs a governed metric definition and daily refresh.

Required guardrails
  • Only products launched within the declared 30-day window
  • Exclude unavailable, restricted, and fulfillment-risk inventory
  • Deduplicate color or size variants at parent-product level
  • Cap one category’s share so broad catalogs stay discoverable
